### 3. Listening Text Summary (`3.1`: *"The Stones That Remember: A Walk Through Karnak"*, p. 178)
- **Silence & Scale:** At the entrance to the **Karnak Temple Complex in Luxor**, the avenue of **ram-headed sphinxes** stretches southward in stillness, carved from sandstone already ancient when Cleopatra walked beside it.
- **Landscape of Accumulated Memory:** Karnak is not a single building but the **longest continuous building project in human history**, built over **more than 2,000 years** from the reign of **Senusret in the Middle Kingdom** to the **Ptolemaic era**. Each pharaoh added, changed, or **inscribed** something, leaving layers of **devotion** and **ambition encoded** in stone.
- **The Hypostyle Hall:** Contains **134 massive columns** decorated from floor to ceiling. It was designed **not simply to impress, but to communicate**: it belonged to the gods, and the pharaoh was their **representative** on earth.
- **Living Heritage:** A "site of memory" carries deep emotional, symbolic, and cultural meaning for a nation (unlike an ordinary old building); it stays alive when each new generation interprets and carries its story forward.

### 3. Reading Text Summary (`3.2`: *"A Journey Through Time — The Grand Egyptian Museum"*, pp. 191–192)
- **Opening & Scale:** Opened to the public on **4 November 2025** near the Pyramids of Giza, the **Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM)** is the **largest archaeological museum in the world**, covering **45,000 square meters** and housing **more than 100,000 artifacts**, including the **complete collection of Tutankhamun** displayed together for the first time in history.
- **The Grand Staircase & Curation:** Features **87 colossal royal statues** arranged in **chronological order**. **Curators** organize the journey from **prehistory** through the great **dynasties**, using **multilingual labels**, interactive screens, and guided tours so the experience is **accessible** to all ages.
- **Education & Conservation:** Unlike museums designed mainly for tourists, GEM offers school trips, workshops, and special programs so Egyptian students connect with their heritage in a **hands-on** way. Inside the museum, **conservation laboratories** allow experts to study and **restore** artifacts using advanced technology.

## Part 3: Section A Grammar Focus — Prepositional Phrases of Place and Time (pp. 183–184 & 197–198)

### 1. Prepositional Phrases of Place (`العبارات الظرفية الدالة على المكان`, pp. 183–184)

| Category | Prepositions | Precise Usage & Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Core Point / Area / Surface** | **`at` / `in` / `on`** | • **`at` (نقطة محددة أو عنوان صغير):** `at the entrance`, `at the top of the hill`, `at the bus stop`.<br>• **`in` (مدن، بلاد، أو داخل حيز مغلق):** `in the bottle`, `in the classroom`, `in Luxor`.<br>• **`on` (ملامسة سطح أفقي أو رأسي أو ضفة نهر):** `on the wall`, `on the Nile`, `on the screen`, `on the ceiling`. |
| **2. Vertical Relative Position** | **`over` / `above`**<br>**`under` / `below`** | • **`over`:** directly above, covering, or moving across (*A plane flew **over** the city*).<br>• **`above`:** higher in level, not necessarily directly vertical (*The temple stands **above** the river*).<br>• **`under`:** directly beneath / covered by (*The cat is **under** the table*).<br>• **`below`:** lower in level (*The valley is **below** the mountain*). |
| **3. Proximity** | **`next to / beside / by`**<br>**`near / close to`** | • **`next to / beside / by`:** directly adjacent (*There is a café **by** the station*).<br>• **`near / close to`:** a short distance away (*near the metro station*). |
| **4. Orientation** | **`between` / `among`**<br>**`in front of` / `behind` / `opposite`** | • **`between`:** between two (or more **distinct, named** items: *between Africa and Asia*).<br>• **`among`:** in the middle of an unspecified group (*among the ruins*).<br>• **`opposite`:** facing each other across a space (*opposite the hotel* — no `to`!). |
| **5. Spatial Extension & Movement** | **`across` / `along` / `through` / `around` / `inside` / `outside` / `beyond` / `from...to` / `towards`** | • **`across`:** from one side to the other (*across the Nile Valley*).<br>• **`along`:** following a line/length (*along the banks of the Nile*).<br>• **`through`:** inside a 3D space/tunnel/gate (*through the gate / through the tunnel*).<br>• **`beyond`:** further away in the distance (*beyond the desert lies the sea*). |

### 2. Prepositional Phrases of Time (`العبارات الظرفية الدالة على الزمن`, pp. 197–198)

| Category | Prepositions | Precise Usage & Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Core Time Prepositions** | **`at` / `on` / `in`** | • **`at` (ساعات، وجبات، عطلات، لحظات محددة):** `at 9 a.m.`, `at night`, `at noon`, `at midnight`, `at Eid`.<br>• **`on` (أيام الأسبوع، تواريخ كاملة، مناسبات يومية):** `on Sunday`, `on Friday morning`, `on the 14th of October`, `on my birthday`, `on a warm summer day`.<br>• **`in` (فترات اليوم، شهور، فصول، سنوات، قرون):** `in the morning`, `in October`, `in summer`, `in 2026`, `in the 21st century`. |
| **2. Sequence & Starting Point** | **`before` / `after` / `since`** | • **`since + starting point in the past`** (used with Present Perfect): *since 1300 BC*.<br>• **Grammar Note:** If `before / after / since` is followed by a **Noun / V-ing**, it is a **preposition** (*After lunch, let's go out*). If followed by a **full clause (`Subject + Verb`)**, it is a **conjunction / linking word**! |
| **3. Duration & Time Periods** | **`for` / `during` / `throughout`** | • **`for + length/amount of time` (عدد الساعات/الأيام/السنين):** *for more than two thousand years / for three hours*.<br>• **`during + noun (name of period/event)`:** *During the reign of Ramesses II / during the match / during the night*.<br>• **`throughout + noun` (طوال الفترة من بدايتها لنهايتها دون انقطاع):** *It rained throughout the night.* |
| **4.
