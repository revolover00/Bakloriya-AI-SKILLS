### 2. Phrases, Prepositions, Derivatives, Synonyms & Confusable Words (`5.3`)
- **Phrases & Idioms:** `Go Green Initiative` (مبادرة «اتحضر للأخضر» للتحول إلى نمط صديق للبيئة), `Green Cities Program` (برنامج المدن الخضراء), `have a role in` (له دور في), `make a big difference` (يُحدث فرقًا كبيرًا), `make cities healthier` (يجعل المدن أكثر صحة), `meet present needs` (يلبي الاحتياجات الحالية), `raise awareness among` (يرفع مستوى الوعي بين), `take significant steps` (يتخذ خطوات هامة).
- **Prepositions:** `focus on` (يركز على), `hear of` (يسمع عن), `know about` (يعرف عن), `move towards` (يتحرك نحو), `throw ... away` (يتخلص من/يرمي), `work on` (يعمل على).
- **Derivatives:**
  - `develop (v)` $\rightarrow$ `development / developer (n)` $\rightarrow$ `developing (نامٍ) / developed (متقدم/مطوَّر) / developmental (تنموي)`
  - `emit (v: يصدر/يطلق)` $\rightarrow$ `emission (انبعاث) / emitter` $\rightarrow$ `emissive (انبعاثي)`
  - `evaluate (v)` $\rightarrow$ `evaluation / evaluator (n)` $\rightarrow$ `evaluative (تقييمي)`
  - `introduce (v)` $\rightarrow$ `introduction (n)` $\rightarrow$ `introductory (تمهيدي/تقديمي)`
  - `recycle (v)` $\rightarrow$ `recycling (n)` $\rightarrow$ `recyclable (قابل لإعادة التدوير)`
- **Synonyms & Antonyms (`5.3`):**
  - `efficiency` = `effectiveness / competence` $\neq$ `inefficiency / wastefulness`
  - `crucial` = `essential / critical` $\neq$ `optional / trivial`
  - `evaluate` = `assess / judge` $\neq$ `ignore / disregard`
  - `maintain` = `keep / preserve / sustain` $\neq$ `neglect / abandon`
  - `waste (v)` = `misuse / squander` $\neq$ `save / conserve`
- **FOCUS on Vocabulary (`5.3`, p. 373):**
  1. **`plan` vs. `conspiracy (plot)` vs. `initiative`:**
     - **plan** (خطة أو ترتيب مسبق لتحقيق هدف، أو خريطة تصميم هندسي).
     - **conspiracy / plot** (مؤامرة سرية غير قانونية أو ضارة).
     - **initiative** (مبادرة إيجابية جديدة تُطلق لمعالجة قضية مجتمعية أو بيئية: *the Go Green Initiative*).
  2. **`process` vs. `operation`:**
     - **process** (سلسلة خطوات طبيعية أو صناعية تدريجية: *recycling process / digestion is a biological process*).
     - **operation** (١- تشغيل جهاز أو نظام `operation of the new solar panels`؛ ٢- عملية جراحية؛ ٣- عملية حسابية؛ ٤- عملية عسكرية أو إنقاذ).
  3. **`evaluate` vs. `estimate`:**
     - **evaluate** (يقيّم جودة أو كفاءة أو نجاح شيء بعد دراسته: *evaluate students' performance*).
     - **estimate** (يقدّر رقمًا أو تكلفة أو مسافة بشكل تقريبي: *estimate the cost of repairs*).

### 3. Listening Text Summary (`5.3`: *"Greening Projects in Egypt"*, p. 371)
- **1. Benban Solar Park (Aswan):** One of the largest solar energy projects in the world; produces clean electricity from sunlight and reduces fossil-fuel pollution.
- **2. The "Go Green Initiative" (`مبادرة اتحضر للأخضر`):** Launched by the **Ministry of Environment** to **raise awareness** among citizens (especially young learners) about protecting natural resources, reducing plastic use, and **recycling** waste.
- **3. Green Cities Program & E-Waste:** Improves cities like **Cairo** (waste recycling, clean transportation, and **energy efficiency** in buildings) and develops **Sharm El-Sheikh as a "green city"** (hotels using solar energy and eco-friendly public transport). New laws and programs also safely manage **electronic waste (e-waste)** and support small recycling businesses.
- **Core Message:** Success toward **sustainability** depends on **both government action and individual behavior change**.

## Part 3: Section B Grammar Focus — Adverbials of Degree, Frequency, and Certainty/Stance (pp. 376–379)

| Category | Key Adverbials | Word-Order Rules & Exam Traps |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Adverbials of Degree (`ظروف الدرجة — تبيّن مدى أو قوة الحدث`)** | `barely / scarcely` (بالكاد), `slightly / marginally` (قليلاً/بشكل هامشي), `partially / partly` (جزئيًا), `moderately` (باعتدال), `considerably / significantly / substantially / greatly / heavily` (بدرجة كبيرة/ملحوظة/جوهرية), `enormously / immensely` (بشكل هائل/للغاية), `completely / entirely / fully / totally` (تمامًا/بالكامل), `largely` (إلى حد كبير) | **4 Positions:**<br>1. **Before an adjective or adverb:** *The wind turbines turn **immensely** fast.*<br>2. **Before the main verb:** *The government **partially** funds solar energy.*<br>3. **Between auxiliary and main verb:** *Carbon emissions **will substantially drop** next year.* / *The factory **is completely powered** by solar.*<br>4. **At the end of the sentence** (for `completely, totally, enormously, immensely, partially`): *We reduced our reliance on oil **completely**.*<br>**TRAP:** NEVER put an adverb between the verb and its direct object ($\times$ *We reduced completely our reliance* / $\times$ *She loves totally her job*). |
| **2. Adverbials of Frequency (`ظروف التكرار`)** | `always, usually, normally, generally, often, frequently, consistently, regularly, sometimes, occasionally, rarely, seldom, hardly ever, never, increasingly` + Definite: `daily, weekly, monthly, annually/yearly, once a week, twice a month` | **Positions:**<br>1. **Before a main verb:** *I **always** study at night.*<br>2. **After `am / is / are / was / were`:** *She **is usually** late.*<br>3. **Between auxiliary/modal and main verb:** *You **must always** wash your hands.*<br>4. **After `not` (`don't/doesn't + adv + verb`):** *I do **not often** watch TV.*<br>5. **Definite frequency (`daily, weekly, twice a week`)** goes at the **end** (or start) of the sentence. |
| **3. Adverbials of Certainty / Stance (`ظروف التأكيد وموقف الكاتب`)** | `absolutely, arguably, certainly, clearly, definitely, evidently, fortunately, frankly/honestly, inevitably (حتمًا), likely, obviously, perhaps/maybe, plainly, possibly, probably, surprisingly, undoubtedly (بلا شك), unfortunately` | Usually placed **at the very beginning of the sentence followed by a comma (`,`)** to express the writer's stance or degree of certainty:<br>• ***Clearly,** individual action alone is not enough.*<br>• ***Undoubtedly,** global temperatures are rising.*<br>• *He has not studied, so he will **inevitably** fail.* |

## Part 4: Section C Writing Skills — Argumentative Paragraph & Informational Leaflet (pp. 400–401)

### 1. Structure of an Argumentative Paragraph (`الفقرة الجدلية`, p. 400)
1. **Topic Sentence:** State your opinion clearly (the main idea of the paragraph).
2. **Supporting Sentences:** Give 2–3 reasons or pieces of evidence (`Moreover, / In addition, / For example,`).
3. **Concession + Refutation (Optional but strengthens argument):** Mention the opposing view (`Some people argue that individual efforts are too small...`), then **refute it** (`However, history has shown that collective action often begins with personal commitment`).
4. **Concluding Sentence:** Restate your opinion in different words without introducing a new idea (`In conclusion, ...`).

### 2. Key Features of an Informational Leaflet (`النشرة الإعلامية التثقيفية`, p. 401)
A compact, single-page or folded printed document designed to educate readers about a specific subject (e.g., *Green Energy Guide*):
1. **Eye-catching headline:** Use a question or bold statement (*"Why are you still paying for Energy the sun gives for free?"*).
2. **Subheadings:** Organize content into clear, easy-to-navigate sections (*What Is Solar Energy? / Why Should Young People Care? / How Do You Get Started?*).
3. **Bullet points:** Present key facts concisely and accessibly.
4. **Call to action (`دعوة للعمل`):** Encourage the reader to do something specific (*"Switch to solar today!" / "Start small: solar-powered chargers are a great first step."*).
5. **Tone & Register + Visuals:** Match the target audience (friendly yet informative for youth) and support text with images/diagrams.
