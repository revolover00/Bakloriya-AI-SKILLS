# Chapter 3 (Unit 1 — Section C): Language in Context & Analytical Writing Skills (pp. 80–86)

## 1. Biased Sentences vs. Neutral Sentences (`الفرق بين الجمل المتحيزة والجمل المحايدة`)

A core reading and writing skill in Unit 1 is distinguishing between **Biased (Subjective/Emotional)** statements and **Neutral (Objective/Fact-based)** statements:

| Type | Characteristics | Textbook Example |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Biased Sentences**<br>*(الجمل المتحيزة)* | • Show a strong personal opinion.<br>• Support one side completely.<br>• Rely on **emotional / evaluative words** and **absolute words** instead of verifiable facts. | *"Social media is a **terrible** waste of time that **completely destroys** teenagers' minds."* |
| **2. Neutral Sentences**<br>*(الجمل المحايدة)* | • Fair, balanced, and objective.<br>• State clear facts or measurable data without choosing sides or showing personal emotions. | *"Millions of teenagers around the world use social media platforms every day."*<br>*"The smart scale shows that the patient's body weight is 75 kilograms."* |

## 2. Persuasive Writing Techniques (`أساليب وتقنيات الكتابة الإقناعية`)

Writers use four specific language tools to convince readers to agree with an opinion, change their mind, or take action:

| Technique | Function & Effect on the Reader | Key Words / Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Rhetorical Questions**<br>*(الأسئلة البلاغية / الخطابية)* | • Questions that do not require an answer.<br>• Used to make the reader think deeply about the topic and guide them to agree with the writer's opinion automatically. | *"Do I really need to check my phone every five minutes?"*<br>*"Do we really want our future generation to be controlled by digital screens?"* |
| **2. Intensifiers**<br>*(كلمات التعزيز / التوكيد)* | • Powerful adverbs/adjectives that make ideas and emotions stronger.<br>• Highlight how important or serious a situation is and **create a sense of urgency**. | `very`, `extremely`, `significantly`, `greatly` |
| **3. Hedging Words**<br>*(ألفاظ التحفظ / التحوط اللغوي)* | • Cautious words used to show uncertainty and avoid $100\%$ fixed claims.<br>• Help the writer present an opinion **without sounding too aggressive or overly generalized**, making the argument sound **realistic, balanced, and scientifically accurate**. | `may`, `might`, `could`, `possibly`, `probably`, `tend to`, `often` |
| **4. Absolute Words**<br>*(ألفاظ الجزم المطلق / اليقين)* | • Total words that state a claim with $100\%$ certainty and allow no exceptions.<br>• Show a strong, forceful stance that leaves no room for doubts or excuses (often a marker of **bias** if unsupported). | `always`, `never`, `every`, `no excuse`, `completely`, `definitely` |

## 3. Structure of an Analytical Paragraph — The `PEEL` Model (`مكونات الفقرة التحليلية`, p. 81)

An **Analytical Paragraph** does not merely describe *what* happened; it explains **how and why** it happened using clear evidence. It follows a 4-part structure (**PEEL: Point – Evidence – Explanation – Link**):

| Component | Role in the Paragraph | Model Analytical Paragraph (Textbook p. 81) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Point (Topic Sentence)**<br>*(الفكرة الرئيسية / الجملة الافتتاحية)* | The first sentence that states the main argument or central conclusion clearly. | *"Regular physical activity is a powerful tool that significantly improves teenagers' mental wellness."* |
| **2. Evidence (Context)**<br>*(الدليل والبرهان العلمي)* | Facts, statistics, or quotes from the text/research that prove and support your point (using neutral, factual data). | *"Recent worldwide statistics show that doing 30 minutes of moderate exercise daily leads to a measurable 20% reduction in stress hormones."* |
| **3. Explanation (Analysis)**<br>*(الشرح والتحليل العميق)* | **The heart of the paragraph:** explains **how and why** the evidence proves your point (cause-and-effect, often using hedging language). | *"This proven benefit occurs because physical movement triggers the release of endorphins, which naturally boost mood and fight daytime fatigue. Consequently, teenagers who maintain active lifestyles tend to build higher emotional resilience."* |
| **4. Link (Conclusion)**<br>*(الربط والختام)* | The final sentence (often starting with `Therefore, / Thus,`) that summarizes the analysis and connects the evidence back to the main point. | *"Therefore, including outdoor sports into a student's routine is an essential foundation for a healthier, stress-free mind."* |

## 4. Applied Reading & Translation Highlights (`Section C`, pp. 82–86)
- **Reading Passage 1 (*Healthy Longevity*):** Three habits to **prolong** (`= lengthen`) life and improve quality of life:
  1. **Diet:** Fill **three-fourths ($\frac{3}{4}$)** of your plate with fruits, vegetables, and whole grains; choose lean meat, poultry, fish, beans; drink water; use a smaller plate to prevent overeating.
  2. **Exercise:** At least **30 minutes a day, 3+ days a week** (or at least 75 minutes total).
  3. **Sleep:** Adults over 18 need **7–9 hours** per night (or a short **20–30 minute nap** if sleep-deprived).
- **Reading Passage 2 (*Rafael Nadal*):** Born in 1986 in Mallorca, Spain; coached from age 4 by his uncle **Toni Nadal** (who changed his style at age 9 to play left-handed although he writes right-handed, while his other uncle Miguel Ángel played football for FC Barcelona). Founded the **Rafa Nadal Foundation** (2008) and **Rafa Nadal Academy** (2016); donated €1 million to Mallorca flood victims (2018) and $250,000 with Roger Federer for Australian bushfire **relief** (`= money raised / assistance`, 2020). Demonstrates determination, resilience, and giving back.
- **Model Translations (`Section C`):**
  1. *"Investing money in renewable energy resources will definitely create millions of new green jobs and boost global economic growth."*
     $\rightarrow$ **إن استثمار الأموال في مصادر الطاقة المتجددة سيخلق بالتأكيد ملايين الوظائف الخضراء الجديدة ويعزز النمو الاقتصادي العالمي.**
  2. *"Online educational platforms offer flexible learning opportunities for millions of adult students who want to improve their career skills."*
     $\rightarrow$ **توفر المنصات التعليمية عبر الإنترنت فرص تعلم مرنة لملايين الطلاب البالغين الذين يرغبون في تحسين مهاراتهم المهنية.**
  3. **«يجب على المراهقين ترتيب أولوياتهم واستخدام عزيمتهم القوية للتوقف عن قضاء ساعات طويلة أمام الشاشات الرقمية يوميًا.»**
     $\rightarrow$ *"Adolescents must prioritize their goals and use their strong determination to stop spending long hours in front of digital screens daily."*
  4. **«تساعد ممارسة التمارين الرياضية بانتظام على تحسين وظائف القلب وتقليل احتمالية الإصابة بالأمراض المزمنة في المستقبل.»**
     $\rightarrow$ *"Doing regular exercises helps to improve heart functions and reduce the probability of developing chronic diseases in the future."*
