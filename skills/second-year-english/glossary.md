# Glossary — 2nd Year Secondary (Egyptian Baccalaureate) English (Term 1 — 2027)

## Core Pedagogical & Critical-Thinking Terms
- **JRE (Justify – Reason – Evidence):** استراتيجية التبرير والسبب والدليل المعتمدة في منهج البكالوريا المصرية للإجابة عن أسئلة التفكير النقدي؛ تتكون من: **Justify** (تحديد الموقف)، **Reason** (تفسير السبب بـ `because/since`)، و**Evidence** (دعم الإجابة بدليل من النص أو الواقع بـ `For example`).
- **PEEL Structure:** هيكل كتابة الفقرة التحليلية (`Analytical Paragraph`) في الوحدة الأولى: **P**oint (الفكرة الرئيسية)، **E**vidence (الدليل)، **E**xplanation (الشرح)، **L**ink (الربط والخاتمة).
- **Hedging (التحوط اللغوي):** استخدام تعبيرات مخففة (`tend to`, `may`, `might`, `can sometimes`, `in many cases`, `It is often argued that`) لتجنب التعميم القاطع وجعل الكتابة الأكاديمية أكثر دقة وموضوعية.
- **Fronting (التقديم للتوكيد):** نقل شبه الجملة الدالة على المكان أو الزمان (`In Egypt,`) أو السبب (`As a result of...,`) أو التناقض (`Unlike...,`) إلى بداية الجملة متبوعة بفاصلة قبل الفاعل.

## Unit 1: Well-being — Living Well in a Complex World (`1.1` – `1.4`)
- **authentic `(adj)`:** أصيل / حقيقي (`genuine and real; not fake or pretended`).
- **collaborative `(adj)`:** تعاوني / تشاركي (`involving two or more people working together for a special purpose`).
- **conduct `(v)`:** يُجري / ينفذ (`to organize and perform a particular activity, e.g., a survey or study`).
- **curate `(v)`:** ينسّق / ينتقي بعناية (`to carefully choose, organize, and present content, especially online or in a museum`).
- **disconnect `(v)`:** ينفصل عن الإنترنت أو العالم الرقمي (`to stop being connected to the internet or digital devices`).
- **fulfilling `(adj)`:** مُشبع نفسيًا / مُرضٍ (`making you feel happy and satisfied because you are doing something useful`).
- **mindfulness `(n)`:** اليقظة الذهنية (`the practice of being aware of your body, mind, and feelings in the present moment`).
- **notification `(n)`:** إشعار رقمي (`a message automatically sent to your phone or computer`).
- **overwhelmed `(adj)`:** مثقَل / مرهَق بالضغوط (`feeling that there is too much to deal with`).
- **peer pressure `(n)`:** ضغط الأقران (`strong influence from people in your age group to behave like them`).
- **polished `(adj)`:** منمَّق / معدَّل ليبدو مثاليًا (`carefully edited or improved to look perfect`).
- **proactive `(adj)`:** استباقي / مبادر (`taking action by causing change and not only reacting to change when it happens`).
- **resilience `(n)`:** المرونة النفسية / القدرة على التعافي (`the ability to recover quickly from difficulties, stress, or change`).
- **screen-free `(adj)`:** خالٍ من الشاشات (`not involving phones, computers, or televisions`).
- **self-esteem `(n)`:** تقدير الذات (`confidence in your own worth or abilities`).
- **validation `(n)`:** استحسان / تأكيد القيمة من الآخرين (`approval or recognition from other people that makes you feel your opinions or worth are accepted`).
- **well-being `(n)`:** الرفاهية والصحة النفسية والجسدية (`the state of feeling healthy, happy, and comfortable`).

## Unit 2: Experiences — Leisure Time (`2.1` – `2.4`)
- **accessible `(adj)`:** متاح / سهل الوصول إليه (`easy to reach, enter, use, or understand`).
- **anxiety `(n)`:** القلق والتوتر النفسي (`an uncomfortable feeling of nervousness or worry about something that is happening or might happen`).
- **cognitive `(adj)`:** معرفي / إدراكي / ذهني (`connected with thinking or conscious mental processes`).
- **excessive `(adj)`:** مفرط / زائد عن الحد (`too much; more than is reasonable or necessary`).
- **exhausting `(adj)`:** مُرهِق جدًا (`making you feel extremely tired`).
- **expressive `(adj)`:** تعبيري (`showing what someone thinks or feels`).
- **frustrating `(adj)`:** مُحبِط (`making you feel annoyed or less confident because you cannot achieve what you want`).
- **hormone `(n)` / endorphins `(n)`:** هرمون / الإندورفين (`chemical substances produced in the body; endorphins improve mood and reduce pain during exercise`).
- **passive `(adj)`:** سلبي / غير متفاعل (`accepting or watching what happens without active response or participation`).
- **productive `(adj)`:** مُنتِج / مثمر (`doing or achieving a lot; leading to good results`).
- **recreation `(n)` / leisure `(n)`:** ترفيه واستجمام / وقت الفراغ (`activities done for enjoyment when you are not working`).
- **sedentary `(adj)`:** خامل / قليل الحركة (`involving little exercise or physical activity; spending a lot of time sitting`).
- **stimulating `(adj)`:** محفّز ذهنيًا / مثير للاهتمام (`encouraging new ideas, enthusiasm, or mental activity`).

## Unit 3: Identity — Echoes of the Past (`3.1` – `3.4`)
- **accumulated `(adj)`:** متراكم (`built up or collected over time`).
- **artifact `(n)`:** قطعة أثرية (`an object made or used by humans in the past, especially one of historical interest`).
- **chronological `(adj)`:** ذو ترتيب زمني متسلسل (`arranged in the order in which events happened over time`).
- **citizen journalism `(n)`:** صحافة المواطن (`news reporting carried out by members of the public, not trained journalists`).
- **conservation `(n)`:** حفظ وترميم الآثار أو البيئة (`the process of protecting and preserving natural or cultural things from damage`).
- **curators `(n)`:** أمناء المتاحف (`people who look after and organize the contents of a museum or exhibition`).
- **decipher `(v)`:** يفك رموزًا أو شفرة (`to figure out, interpret, or decode ancient writing or symbols`).
- **devotion `(n)`:** إخلاص / تفانٍ (`strong love and care for someone or something`).
- **digital archive `(n)`:** أرشيف رقمي (`a collection of historical materials stored and accessible online`).
- **documentation `(n)`:** توثيق بالمستندات (`the process of recording and keeping information, evidence, or details`).
- **dynasties `(n)`:** الأسر أو السلالات الحاكمة (`a series of rulers or kings from the same family who rule a country over a long period`).
- **encoded `(adj)`:** مشفَّر / مرمَّز (`converted into signs or symbols to carry meaning`).
- **excavation `(n)`:** تنقيب عن الآثار / حفر علمي (`the activity of digging to discover old objects or buildings`).
- **fragile `(adj)`:** هش / سريع الزوال أو الكسر (`easily damaged or broken`).
- **inscribed `(adj)`:** منقوش / محفور (`written or carved into a surface`).
- **media literacy `(n)`:** التثقيف والوعي الإعلامي (`the ability to critically read and evaluate media content`).
- **multilingual `(adj)`:** متعدد اللغات (`able to speak, use, or write in more than one language`).
- **ram-headed sphinx `(n)`:** تمثال أبو الهول برأس كبش (`an ancient Egyptian statue with a lion's body and a ram's head`).
- **real-time documentation `(n)`:** توثيق فوري لحظة بلحظة (`recording and sharing events as they happen, without delay`).
- **selective framing `(n)`:** التأطير الانتقائي (`choosing which details to include or exclude to shape the audience's understanding`).
- **submerged `(adj)`:** مغمور تحت الماء (`covered or hidden under water`).
- **verify `(v)`:** يتحقق من صحة / يثبت (`to check or prove that something is true, correct, or accurate`).

## Unit 4: Consumption and Choice — The Power of Choice (`4.1` – `4.4`)
- **belonging `(n)`:** الشعور بالانتماء (`the feeling of being part of a group`).
- **best-before date `(n)`:** تاريخ الأفضل للاستهلاك قبل — يتعلق بالجودة والطعم وليس الأمان (`date showing until when food keeps its best taste and quality`).
- **brand `(n)`:** علامة تجارية (`a well-known name and identity associated with a company's products or services`).
- **concentration `(n)`:** تركيز ذهني (`the ability to focus mentally on a special task`).
- **confusion `(n)`:** ارتباك / خلط (`the state of being unable to understand something clearly`).
- **countdown `(n)`:** عدّ تنازلي (`a timer showing how much time is left`).
- **fall for `(phr. v)`:** ينخدع بـ / يقع في فخ (`to be tricked into believing something`).
- **generosity `(n)`:** الكرم والسخاء (`being kind and willing to give a lot to others`).
- **leftover `(n/adj)`:** بقايا الطعام الصالحة للأكل بعد الوجبة (`food remains that haven't been eaten after a meal`).
- **logo `(n)`:** شعار الشركة المرسوم (`a picture or symbol used to represent a company`).
- **loss aversion `(n phrase)`:** النفور من الخسارة / كراهية الخسارة (`avoiding loss more than gaining; caring more about not losing a discount than acquiring an equal gain`).
- **loyalty `(n)`:** الولاء للعلامة التجارية (`always choosing the same brand and not wanting to change`).
- **purchase `(v/n)`:** يشتري / عملية شراء (`to buy something`).
- **scroll `(v)`:** يمرر الشاشة لأعلى أو لأسفل (`to move through digital content on a screen, typically by moving up or down`).
- **storage `(n)`:** التخزين / مساحة التخزين (`keeping things in a safe place until you need them`).
- **urgency `(n)`:** الإلحاح والضرورة الملحة (`a feeling that something must be done quickly`).
- **use-by date `(n)`:** تاريخ انتهاء الصلاحية النهائي للأمان الغذائي (`the final date by which food is safe to eat`).

## Unit 5: Sharing the Planet — The Greenhouse Effect (`5.1` – `5.4`)
- **acidification `(n)`:** تحمُّض المحيطات أو التربة (`the process of becoming more acidic`).
- **achievable `(adj)`:** قابل للتحقيق (`capable of being achieved`).
- **affordable `(adj)`:** بأسعار معقولة في متناول الجميع (`cheap enough for most people to afford`).
- **alarming rate `(n)`:** معدل ينذر بالخطر (`a speed or level that causes worry because it is increasing quickly`).
- **biodiversity `(n)`:** التنوع البيولوجي (`the variety of plant and animal life`).
- **bleaching `(n)`:** ابيضاض الشعاب المرجانية وفقدان لونها (`the process of losing color due to ocean warming and acidification`).
- **consensus `(n)`:** إجماع علمي أو عام (`general agreement among a group of people`).
- **determination `(n)`:** إصرار وعزيمة (`the quality of trying to do something even when it is difficult`).
- **efficiency `(n)`:** الكفاءة وترشيد الموارد (`the ability to perform a task with minimum waste of time and resources`).
- **emissions `(n)`:** الانبعاثات الغازية الملوثة (`gases released into the atmosphere, especially those causing pollution`).
- **fertile `(adj)`:** خصب (`able to produce many plants or crops`).
- **fossil fuel `(n)`:** الوقود الأحفوري كالفحم والبترول والغاز (`a fuel such as coal or oil produced by the gradual decaying of animals or plants over millions of years`).
- **fraction `(n)`:** جزء ضئيل أو كسر (`a small part or amount of something`).
- **harness `(v)`:** يسخّر / يستغل طاقة طبيعية (`to capture and use a natural force such as wind or sunlight to produce power`).
- **initiative `(n)`:** مبادرة (`a plan or program introduced to achieve a specific goal`).
- **innovation `(n)` / innovators `(n)`:** ابتكار / مبتكرون (`the introduction of new ideas or methods / people who introduce them`).
- **intensity `(n)`:** شدة / حدة (`the strength or degree of something`).
- **intermittency `(n)`:** التقطُّع وعدم الاستمرارية في طاقة الشمس والرياح (`coming and going at intervals; not continuous`).
- **investment `(n)`:** استثمار مالي (`the use of money to get a profit or make a business activity successful`).
- **leapfrog `(v)`:** يتخطى مرحلة تقليدية مباشرة إلى التقنية الأحدث (`to bypass an older stage of technology such as fossil fuels and move directly to renewables`).
- **recycling `(n)`:** إعادة التدوير (`the process of converting waste materials into new, reusable products`).
- **renewable `(adj)`:** متجدد (`easily replaced because there is a large supply of it`).
- **sustainability `(n)`:** الاستدامة (`the ability to meet present needs without harming future generations`).
